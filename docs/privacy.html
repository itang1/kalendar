Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Kalendar is designed to work entirely on your device (and, on iPhone and iPad, your own iCloud account). It is built so that your use of the app is nobody's business but your own. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app, the Android app, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. Kalendar does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server we control. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and makes no network requests other than the iCloud syncing described below.

Your notes on iPhone and iPad

Any notes you add to a day are stored on your device and synced through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, using your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices signed into the same Apple ID. They are never sent to us or to any other service. If you uninstall the app from one device, your notes remain in iCloud and are restored if you reinstall or open the app on another device signed into the same account.

Your notes on Android

Any notes you add are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any other service. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, they are included in that backup to your Google account, the same as other app data, which lets them come back when you set up a new phone.

Notifications

If you turn on solemnity notifications, they are scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved, and nothing about them leaves your device.

This website

This website, including the calendar you can browse in your browser, counts page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It records the page viewed, the site that linked to it, and general details such as browser, screen size, and country. It uses no cookies and does not store your IP address or anything that identifies you. The browser calendar does not let you add notes and stores nothing about you. The iPhone, iPad, and Android apps do not use GoatCounter or any other analytics.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks that collect data.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
